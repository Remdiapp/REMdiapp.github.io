The perfect moment to wake

REMdi monitors your sleep stages in real time and waits for the lightest phase within your wake window — when your body is already halfway to consciousness. No more wrenching yourself from deep sleep.

II

Smarter every night

Over weeks and months, REMdi builds a precise map of your personal sleep architecture — your unique rhythms, sensitivities, and patterns. The longer you use it, the more attuned it becomes to you.

III

Works with what you wear

Connect Oura Ring, Whoop, or Apple Watch. REMdi reads biometric data from whichever device you already trust — heart rate variability, movement, SpO₂ — and synthesises it into a single, intelligent signal.
